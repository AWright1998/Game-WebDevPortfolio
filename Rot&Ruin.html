Rot & Ruin: The Asylum

A survival horror project inspired by classic Resident Evil, focused on atmosphere, deliberate pacing, exploration, and tension-driven gameplay.

Unity Survival Horror Honours Project C#

Gameplay

Overview

Rot & Ruin is a survival horror experience built around tension, vulnerability, and environmental storytelling. Inspired by classic survival horror design, the project focuses on careful exploration, limited resources, and creating a constant sense of unease.

My Role

  • Gameplay programming using C# in Unity
  • Level design and environment layout
  • Atmosphere and pacing design
  • Core survival horror systems development

Key Features

  • Classic survival horror gameplay inspiration
  • Exploration-driven level design
  • Tension-based pacing and player vulnerability
  • Atmospheric environments and lighting

Development Process

This project focused on how level layout and pacing influence player emotion. I designed environments to build tension through uncertainty, limited visibility, and controlled progression. Gameplay systems were developed to reinforce a slower, more deliberate player experience.

What I Learned

  • How environment design influences player tension
  • How pacing affects horror gameplay
  • How to structure gameplay systems around vulnerability
