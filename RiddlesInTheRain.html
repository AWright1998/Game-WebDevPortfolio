Project Overview

A case study focused on investigation gameplay, player navigation, and narrative support through level design.

Riddles In The Rain was developed as part of the Portfolio Production module during university. The project was inspired by investigative games such as L.A. Noire, with an emphasis on player observation, narrative progression, and environmental storytelling.

The aim was to build a game experience where the player engages with the world through exploration, clue gathering, and space-driven progression. As part of the development team, my role focused on level design.

Project Context

The project explored how environment design can support investigation-based gameplay.

Design Goal

Create a narrative-driven environment that supports investigative gameplay and encourages the player to move through the space with purpose, attention, and curiosity.

Player Experience

The intended experience was one of controlled discovery, where the player is guided through the environment by layout, visual cues, and the logic of the investigation itself.

My Role

My contribution focused primarily on the structure and readability of the game space.

Responsibilities

  • Level design and environment layout
  • Player flow and progression design
  • Supporting narrative through space and layout
  • Collaboration within a team-based project

Focus Area

My work centered on designing environments that helped guide the player naturally through investigation-based gameplay, while also reinforcing the tone and pacing of the narrative.

Level Design Approach

The layout needed to support both exploration and story progression without feeling overly restrictive.

Flow

Guiding Progression

I designed spaces to help guide the player through the investigation in a readable way, using layout and environmental structure to support movement and discovery.

Story

Environmental Storytelling

The environment was intended to support the narrative, helping communicate context and mood while reinforcing the investigative theme of the project.

Pacing

Player Attention

The design approach aimed to control where the player looked, moved, and paused, helping maintain engagement and support clue-driven interaction.

Development Process

Working within a team meant aligning level design with the wider goals of the project.

As part of a team project, the development process required both collaboration and clarity of role. My contribution focused on building environments that supported the game’s investigative structure, ensuring the player’s path through the level felt intentional and connected to the narrative.

This involved thinking carefully about progression, environmental readability, and how space can support a player’s understanding of objectives without relying too heavily on direct instruction.

Outcome & Learning

The project helped strengthen my understanding of how space and narrative work together in game design.

What I Learned

  • How level design supports narrative-driven gameplay
  • How to guide players through investigation using environment design
  • How to collaborate effectively in a team-based project
  • How to design spaces that reinforce gameplay objectives

Key Takeaway

This project reinforced my interest in level design and showed how environment layout can shape player understanding, pacing, and engagement in narrative-driven experiences.
