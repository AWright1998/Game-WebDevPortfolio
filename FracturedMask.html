Fractured Mask

A first-person horror puzzle game combining exploration, puzzles, and atmosphere to create a tense and immersive player experience.

Unity Game Jam First-Person Horror Puzzle Design

Gameplay

Overview

Fractured Mask is a first-person horror puzzle experience developed with a focus on atmosphere, exploration, and puzzle-based progression. The project combines environmental tension with player interaction to create a compact but immersive horror experience.

My Role

  • Gameplay implementation in Unity
  • Puzzle design and interaction flow
  • Environmental setup and atmosphere
  • Rapid prototyping and iteration

Key Features

  • First-person horror perspective
  • Puzzle-based gameplay progression
  • Atmospheric exploration
  • Short-form focused experience

Development Process

This project focused on combining mood and puzzle-solving into a short, effective gameplay experience. I aimed to make the environment feel unsettling while ensuring the puzzle progression remained readable and engaging.

What I Learned

  • How to combine puzzle structure with horror atmosphere
  • How small environmental details affect immersion
  • How to keep a short experience cohesive and focused
