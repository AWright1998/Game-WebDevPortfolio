PROJECT FILE • ARCADE SHOOTER

Take Them Down

An on-rails shooter inspired by 90s arcade games, built around fast pacing, enemy pressure, and responsive action gameplay.

Unity On-Rails Shooter Arcade C#

Overview

Take Them Down is an arcade-inspired on-rails shooter designed to capture the fast, immediate feel of classic 90s arcade action. The focus of the project was readable combat, quick reactions, and maintaining a strong sense of momentum throughout the experience.

My Role

  • Gameplay implementation in Unity
  • Enemy encounter setup
  • Combat pacing and flow
  • Action-focused gameplay design

Key Features

  • On-rails movement structure
  • Arcade-inspired shooting gameplay
  • Fast enemy encounters
  • Responsive player feedback

Development Process

This project let me explore how action games create intensity through speed, pressure, and visual clarity. I focused on making encounters readable while maintaining the energy expected from an arcade-inspired shooter.

What I Learned

  • How pacing affects action gameplay
  • How encounter structure shapes player pressure
  • How to balance readability with speed
