About Aidan

A portfolio focused on horror, retro-inspired design, gameplay systems, and interactive web-based experiences.

I’m Aidan Wright, a game developer with a strong focus on Unity and a passion for building games that create tension, atmosphere, and memorable player experiences. My work is especially inspired by horror design, retro gameplay ideas, immersive level design, and systems-driven gameplay.

I enjoy creating experiences that feel deliberate and engaging, whether that means careful survival horror pacing, arcade-inspired action, first-person puzzle-based gameplay, narrative-driven team projects, or web-based simulation games.

My background combines technical game development with digital design and web development, giving me a strong foundation in gameplay creation, interactive design, front-end development, and portfolio presentation.
